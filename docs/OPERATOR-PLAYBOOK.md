## 4. The copy rules (the law of this funnel)

People leave when the words are too "spicy". This funnel speaks like a kind teacher, not a marketer.

1. Grade 4 reading level. Most sentences under 12 words.
2. Show the steps: "Step 1, Step 2, Step 3".
3. Say exactly what they get: "4 short videos. 1 class plan." Never "a transformational system".
4. No income numbers, no "six figures", no fake urgency, no countdown timers, no "only 3 left".
5. No hype words: secret, hack, explode, crush, insane, guaranteed income, passive income.
6. Use your real words: joie, connexion, communauté, "lead with love".
7. First person. You speak: "je", "I".
8. French first, for Quebec: "courriel", "cours", "atelier". *Vous*.
9. No em dashes or en dashes. Use a period or a comma.
10. Every promise matches what the buyer receives.
11. Footer: results depend on each person; no income is promised.

**The test:** would a tired teacher understand this in 3 seconds on her phone?

## 5. Before we launch: decisions

### 5.1 Two issues found while building (decide first)

**Issue 1: "Instant access" vs manual access.**
The pages say *Accès immédiat / instant access* and *"Tout de suite après l'achat, vous recevez un courriel"*. But Kajabi is not connected yet, so you give access by hand. A buyer who pays at 11 pm and waits until morning will feel let down, and copy rule 10 says every promise must match.

- **Option A (recommended for the first test):** change the words to *"Votre accès arrive par courriel en moins de 24 heures"* / *"Your access arrives by email within 24 hours"*, and do Job 1 every day, weekends too.
- **Option B (before we scale past about 5 buyers a day):** Wali connects payments to Kajabi so access is automatic. Then the words go back to "instant".

**Issue 2: The language of the videos.**
French is the default page. But the 4 front-end videos come from *Mom and Baby Dance Teacher Training*, which looks English only. The FAQ says the kit is in both languages.

- **Option A:** record the 4 short videos again in French (best for Quebec).
- **Option B:** add French subtitles, and say so on the page: *"Vidéos en anglais, sous-titres en français."*
- **Option C:** start ads in English Canada only, and add Quebec when French videos are ready.

## 6. Kajabi: baby steps

Kajabi changes its menus sometimes. If what you see does not match, send Claude a screenshot.

### 6.1 Before you start

- Log in to Kajabi as the admin.
- Check your plan has room for **4 new products** and **4 new offers** (Settings, then your plan or billing page).
- Have ready: the 4 front-end videos, 1 class plan (PDF), 1 playlist (link or PDF), the room and safety checklist (PDF), the 13 props guide (PDF).

### 6.2 Build the 4 products

Do not rebuild lessons. **Duplicate** a course you already have, then delete what is not in the kit.

**Product 1: Votre premier cours maman-bébé / Your First Mom and Baby Dance Class**
1. Go to **Products**. Open the list of your courses.
2. Find *Mom and Baby Dance Teacher Training*. Open its **⋯** menu and choose **Duplicate**.
3. Rename the copy: *Votre premier cours maman-bébé / Your First Mom and Baby Dance Class*.
4. Keep only these lessons: **Principles**, **Holds**, **Babywearing**, **First Class and Discussion**. Delete everything else in the copy. (Your original course is not touched.)
5. Add one lesson called **Your class kit**. Upload the class plan PDF, the playlist and the room and safety checklist as downloads.
6. Change the product image to the kit cover (Wali sends it).
7. Click into each lesson once to check it plays.

**Product 2: 13 objets / 13 Simple Props**
1. Products, then **New product**, then a simple course (Mini course or Blank).
2. One lesson: *Your 13 props guide*. Upload the PDF made from the Object Exploration module.

**Product 3: Dansez avec bébé en porte-bébé / Dancing With Babies in Carriers**
1. Duplicate *Mom and Baby Dance Teacher Training* again.
2. Keep only **Dancing with Babies in Carriers** and **Choreography with Babies in Carriers**.
3. Rename. This one product serves both bump 2 and upsell 1.

**Product 4: Une saison complète / A Full Season of Classes [CONFIRM]**
1. Duplicate *Mom and Baby Dance Teacher Training*.
2. Keep only the **Class Planning** module: Class Plans, Music, Playlists.
3. Rename.

**Create one offer (repeat 4 times):**
1. Go to **Sales**, then **Offers**, then **New offer**.
2. Name it exactly as in the table (for example *MD Kit 27*).
3. Choose the product it opens.
4. Price: one payment, in CAD, the real price.
5. Save. Do not add it to your store page.

**Create the tags (once):**
1. Go to **Contacts**, then **Tags** (or add them on the first contact; Kajabi creates the tag when you type it).
2. Create each tag from the table above.

Tags do not give access by themselves. **The offer gives access.** Tags help you sort buyers and send emails later.

### 6.4 Give access to a buyer (Job 1, every day)

Do this once a day, at the same time. About 1 to 2 minutes per buyer.

1. Open the **sales dashboard** (or Stripe, then **Payments**, until the dashboard is ready). Copy the new buyers since yesterday: name, email, what they bought, language. Paste them to Claude. Claude makes your checklist.
2. In Kajabi, go to **Contacts**, then **People**. Search the buyer's email.
   - Not there? Click **Add contact** (or *Add person*). Enter name and email. Save.
3. Open her profile. Find **Offers**, then click **Grant offer**.
4. Choose the offer from the map (for example *MD Kit 27*). If Kajabi asks whether to send the access email, choose **yes**.
5. Repeat step 4 for each bump or upsell she bought.
6. In her profile, add the tags from the map.
7. Back in the dashboard, mark the order **done**.

**Check:** the buyer gets an email from Kajabi to log in. If she writes "I did not get it", check her spam, then resend from her profile in Kajabi.

### 6.5 Refunds

1. Refund in Stripe (Wali shows you once).
2. In Kajabi, open her profile. Under Offers, **revoke** the offer.
3. Add the tag `md-refunded`.
4. Reply kindly. Thank her for trying.

### 6.6 Later: automatic access

When you have more than about 5 buyers a day, or before scaling ads, Wali connects payments to Kajabi (a "grant offer" automation). Then Job 1 becomes a 2-minute check instead of a task.

### 8.5 Decision rules (starting rules, we adjust after week 2)

**Break-even cost per buyer = your average order value** (about CA$44 if the bumps and upsells perform as planned; your dashboard shows the real number).

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| An ad spent 2× break-even (about CA$90) and has 0 sales | Pause that ad. |
| An ad set is under break-even for 3 days, with 3 or more sales | Raise its budget by 20%. Wait 3 days before the next change. |
| An ad set is above break-even for 5 days | Swap in 2 new ads. Do not change the budget. |
| People see the same ad more than 3 times (frequency) | Add new ads. |
| Bump 1 under 15% or bump 2 under 8% | Ask Claude for new bump headlines. Do not touch the ads. |
| Many clicks, few buyers | The page is the problem, not the ad. Tell Wali. |

Never change one ad set more than once in 3 days. Meta needs time to learn.
