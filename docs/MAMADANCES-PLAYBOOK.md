## 2. Avatar (one buyer for this funnel)

**Who:** a woman, 28 to 50, who already teaches movement. Dance teacher, yoga teacher, dance therapist, early childhood educator, or studio owner. Often a mother herself. French-speaking Quebec first, then English Canada, France, Belgium, Switzerland, US.

**Where she is now**
- She loves teaching but her income is up and down.
- She has free hours in the day, or a studio that sits empty in the morning.
- She has thought "I could teach moms and babies" but does not know where to start.
- She worries: Is it safe? What do I do for 45 minutes? What music? Will anyone come?

**What she wants:** one new class she feels ready to teach, that people pay for, that she loves.

**What she does not want:** another long theory course. A business guru. Hype.

**Not the buyer here:** new mothers who want to attend a class. They are Eryn's class customers, not this funnel's customers. Do not target them in ads for this funnel.

## 6. Copy rules (the law of this funnel)

People leave when copy is too "spicy". This funnel speaks like a kind teacher, not a marketer.

1. Grade 4 reading level. Check with a readability tool. Target Flesch-Kincaid grade 4 to 5 in English. Same simplicity in French.
2. One idea per sentence. Most sentences under 12 words.
3. Show the steps. "Step 1, Step 2, Step 3." People buy what they can picture doing.
4. Say what they get, exactly. "4 short videos. 1 class plan. 1 playlist. 1 checklist." Not "a transformational system."
5. No income numbers, no "six figures", no fake urgency, no countdown timers, no "only 3 left".
6. No hype words: secret, hack, explode, crush, insane, guaranteed income, passive income.
7. Use her real words where you can: joie, connexion, communauté, "lead with love".
8. First person. Eryn speaks: "je", "I".
9. French first, written for Quebec: "courriel", "cours", "atelier". Vouvoiement ("vous").
10. No em dashes or en dashes (the build rejects them). Use a period or a comma.
11. Every promise must match what the buyer actually receives.
12. Footer earnings line: results depend on each person; no income is promised.

Test every headline with one question: *Would a tired teacher understand this in 3 seconds on her phone?*
